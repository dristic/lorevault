## When you need a commercial license

The AGPL requires that any modified version deployed as a network service must have its full
source code made available to users of that service. You need a commercial license if you:

- Run LoreVault as a hosted/SaaS service and cannot or do not wish to release your modifications
- Embed LoreVault crates in a proprietary product that is not itself AGPL-licensed
- Distribute LoreVault as part of an appliance or managed offering

Self-hosters who run an unmodified (or AGPL-compliant) version for their own organization do
**not** need a commercial license.
